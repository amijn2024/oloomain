Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At ProTools, accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by ProTools and how we use it.

Google DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is one of a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet. However, visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at the following URL – https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Advertising Partners Privacy Policies

Third-party ad servers or ad networks uses technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on ProTools, which are sent directly to users' browser. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see on websites that you visit.

Note that ProTools has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

Google Advertising Cookies and Choices

When Google advertising is enabled on ProTools, third-party vendors including Google may use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor’s prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to ProTools and/or other sites on the Internet.

Visitors can review or manage personalized advertising choices through Google Ads Settings. Depending on location and applicable requirements, additional consent choices may also be presented before advertising cookies are used.

CCPA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the CCPA, among other rights, consumers have the right to:

  • Request that a business that collects a consumer's personal data disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data that a business has collected about consumers.
  • Request that a business delete any personal data about the consumer that a business has collected.
  • Request that a business that sells a consumer's personal data, not sell the consumer's personal data.

Children's Information (COPPA)

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. ProTools does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.
