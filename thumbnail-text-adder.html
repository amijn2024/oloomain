Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this page.

What is a thumbnail text adder?

It is a simple image editor focused on placing text over an image for thumbnails, profile graphics, or social posts.

How much text should I put on a thumbnail?

Short, readable phrases are usually easier to scan than long paragraphs. Keep the message focused on the image purpose.

Can I use the output for profile pictures?

Yes. The tool can be used for images where a short text label or title is useful, including profile graphics.

Should I check readability on mobile?

Yes. Many thumbnails and social images are viewed at small sizes, so previewing the result at a reduced size helps confirm the text is readable.
