Helpful answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about this tool and its practical use.

What is a thumbnail text adder?

It is a simple image editor for placing text over an image, especially for thumbnails, profile graphics, and social posts.

How much text should I put on a thumbnail?

Short phrases are usually easier to scan than long paragraphs. Focus on one clear message.

How can I keep text readable?

Use strong contrast, sufficient size, simple wording, and enough spacing between the text and important image details.

Should I preview the final image before publishing?

Yes. Check it at the actual or approximate display size because text that looks clear while editing may become difficult to read after resizing.
