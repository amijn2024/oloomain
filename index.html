Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this page.

What does the AI Background Remover do?

It separates the main subject from the background so you can create an image with a transparent background.

What images work best?

Images with a clearly visible subject and good contrast between the subject and background generally produce the cleanest result.

What is an Instagram grid maker?

It divides one image into multiple tiles that can be posted separately to create a larger visual across an Instagram profile grid.

Which grid sizes can I create?

The tool is designed for common three-column Instagram layouts such as 3x1, 3x2, 3x3, and 3x4.

Why convert an image format?

Different formats balance quality, transparency, compatibility, and file size differently. Converting helps match the requirements of a website or platform.

Why compress images?

Smaller images can load faster and are easier to upload or share, especially when a service has a file-size limit.

What is a hashtag generator used for?

It helps brainstorm topical words and phrases that can be used as hashtags or content tags.

Should I use every generated hashtag?

No. Use only hashtags that accurately describe the content and fit the platform and audience.

Are these real font files?

No. Most social font generators use Unicode characters that resemble different font styles rather than installing a new font.

Will every style work everywhere?

Not always. Support varies by device, operating system, browser, and platform, so you should preview the text before publishing.

What is an image cropper?

An image cropper lets you keep a selected area of a picture while removing the outer portions.

Does cropping reduce image dimensions?

Usually yes. The exported image dimensions reflect the selected crop area unless the tool also resizes the result.

What does a case converter do?

It changes capitalization without requiring you to retype the text manually.

What is title case?

Title case capitalizes the main words in a title while commonly leaving certain short connecting words lowercase, depending on the style rules used.

Why do social captions lose spacing?

Some apps trim blank lines or handle whitespace differently when text is pasted, which can make carefully formatted captions look compressed.

What does a caption spacer do?

It helps create copyable spacing so sections of a caption remain easier to separate after pasting into supported platforms.

How does a script length calculator estimate time?

It compares the number of words in a script with an assumed speaking rate, usually measured in words per minute.

Is the estimated duration exact?

No. Pauses, emphasis, transitions, demonstrations, and individual speaking style can make the recorded video shorter or longer.

What is a thumbnail text adder?

It is a simple image editor focused on placing text over an image for thumbnails, profile graphics, or social posts.

How much text should I put on a thumbnail?

Short, readable phrases are usually easier to scan than long paragraphs. Keep the message focused on the image purpose.
