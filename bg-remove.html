Helpful answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this page.

What does the AI Background Remover do?

It separates the main subject from the background so you can create an image with a transparent background.

What images work best?

Images with a clearly visible subject and good contrast between the subject and background generally produce the cleanest result.

Can I use the result for social media or product images?

Yes. The transparent output can be reused in thumbnails, profile graphics, product listings, presentations, and social posts.

Do I need to install software?

No. The tool runs from a modern web browser, although processing may use browser-delivered libraries required by the tool.
