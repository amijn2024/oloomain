How to Use the Image Converter

  1. Upload your image – Click "Select an Image" and choose a file from your device (JPG, PNG, or WebP).

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this page.

Why convert an image format?

Different formats balance quality, transparency, compatibility, and file size differently. Converting helps match the requirements of a website or platform.

Why compress images?

Smaller images can load faster and are easier to upload or share, especially when a service has a file-size limit.

Which format should I choose?

JPG is common for photos, PNG is useful when transparency is required, and WebP often provides efficient web delivery.

Does compression always reduce quality?

Some compression methods trade a small amount of visual quality for a much smaller file. The best setting depends on how the image will be used.
