How to Use the Image Converter

  1. Upload your image – Click "Select an Image" and choose a file from your device (JPG, PNG, or WebP).
  2. Preview instantly – See your original image right inside the tool before making changes.
  3. Choose output format – Select WebP (recommended for web), JPEG, or PNG from the dropdown.
  4. Adjust quality & dimensions – Drag the quality slider (10–100%) for WebP/JPEG and optionally set new width/height in pixels.
  5. Convert & download – Hit the button and your converted, resized image saves directly to your device – no waiting, no server upload.

💡 All processing happens locally in your browser for maximum speed and privacy.

How do I convert an image to WebP, JPG, or PNG?

Upload your image, choose the desired output format (WebP, JPEG, or PNG), adjust quality and dimensions if needed, then click 'Convert & Download'. Everything happens locally in your browser.

Helpful answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about this tool and its practical use.

Why would I convert an image format?

Different formats provide different combinations of compatibility, transparency, compression efficiency, and image quality.

Which format is best for photographs?

JPG is widely supported and is commonly used for photographs. WebP can also be efficient for web delivery when the destination supports it.

When should I use PNG?

PNG is useful when you need transparency or lossless image storage, especially for graphics with sharp edges or limited colors.

Does compressing an image always reduce quality?

Lossy compression can reduce visual quality, while lossless methods aim to preserve the original image data. The best choice depends on the destination.
