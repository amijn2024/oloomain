Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this page.

What does a case converter do?

It changes capitalization without requiring you to retype the text manually.

What is title case?

Title case capitalizes the main words in a title while commonly leaving certain short connecting words lowercase, depending on the style rules used.

What is sentence case?

Sentence case generally capitalizes the beginning of a sentence while keeping most other letters lowercase.

Does the tool show text statistics?

Yes. The page includes live character, word, and line counts to help you review the text while editing.
