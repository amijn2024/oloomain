Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about this page.

How does a script length calculator estimate time?

It compares the number of words in a script with an assumed speaking rate, usually measured in words per minute.

Is the estimated duration exact?

No. Pauses, emphasis, transitions, demonstrations, and individual speaking style can make the recorded video shorter or longer.

What speaking speed should I use?

Use a pace close to your normal delivery. If you are unsure, compare multiple speeds and leave extra time for pauses or on-screen actions.

Can this help with TikTok, Reels, and Shorts?

Yes. It is useful whenever you need a quick estimate of spoken duration before recording a short-form or longer video.
